## The subject

**CELAM** — the Consejo Episcopal Latinoamericano y Caribeño (founded 1955,
Bogotá HQ): the council coordinating Latin America's 22 national bishops'
conferences. Per its statutes an organ of "contact, communion and service" —
coordinative, not magisterial. Five papally convoked **General Conferences**
(Rio 1955, Medellín 1968, Puebla 1979, Santo Domingo 1992, Aparecida 2007)
versus CELAM's own periodic **General Assemblies** (40th in 2025 — the
70th-anniversary return to Rio). Current presidency: Cardinal Jaime Spengler
(2023–2027); secretary general Lizardo Estrada.
